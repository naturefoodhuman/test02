## How to prove MTP speedup
1. Run a fixed prompt with MTP/spec flags enabled; capture tok/s and acceptance/draft log lines.
2. Run the same prompt with MTP/spec flags disabled; capture tok/s.
3. Compare time-to-first-token, decode tok/s, total tokens/sec, and acceptance rate.
4. For llama.cpp MTP, verify --spec-type draft-mtp and --spec-draft-n-max are present.
